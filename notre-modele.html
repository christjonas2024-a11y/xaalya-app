Our model and our independence

Xaalya compares money transfer operators on the only criterion that matters to the recipient: the net amount they receive, fees and exchange rate included. For that ranking to have value, it must be neutral.

This page explains how we ensure it, what we refuse, and how you can verify it.

Last updated: 30 October 2026.

1. Our model, in one sentence

Xaalya is funded by the advertising of financial advertisers — banks, insurers, savings fintechs, real estate players — and never by money transfer operators.

These ads are clearly labelled 'Partner', in the newsletter and on the site. They do not fund the ranking: they fund research time, rate collection and the writing of comparisons.

2. What we do not do

This section is the most important. It lists the practices we forbid ourselves, and which are common in the comparison industry.

We receive no affiliate commission from operators.

No outbound click, no account opening, no transaction earns us a single cent from a money transfer operator.

We accept no advertising from a money transfer operator.

No banner, no sponsorship, no mention — whatever the amount offered.

We sell no position in any ranking.

The default ranking is generated by descending order of net amount received. No commercial agreement can change it.

We do not monetise our readers' personal data.

No email address is required to use the comparator. No tracking tool is installed on this site.

We do not hide exchange rate margins behind '0 fees'.

Our method includes the exchange rate margin in the total cost, and we explain it.

3. How the ranking is calculated

The default ranking of each corridor is based on a single figure: the net amount received by the recipient , for a given amount sent.

What is included

  • Fees displayed by the operator
  • Exchange rate margin (difference from the interbank rate)
  • Receiving fees, where applicable

What is not included

  • A commercial agreement (there are none)
  • The operator's reputation
  • The volume of traffic generated
  • The amount of their advertising spend

Update frequency. Rates are collected daily and tables are updated. The date of the latest collection is displayed under the comparison form.

4. Separation between editorial and commercial

The writing of comparisons, operator profiles, guides and studies is independent of the commercial function.

What the advertiser approves: their banner, visual, text, link. That is all.

What they do not approve: the ranking, an operator's rating, the content of a study.

Advertising placements never appear inside a comparison table, above the ranking, or in a block of figures.

5. Errors and corrections

A comparator claiming never to be wrong is not credible. Rates change, limits evolve, operators modify their pricing.

  • Any reported error is verified within 5 business days.
  • When confirmed, it is corrected and the correction is dated and visible.
  • Substantial corrections are mentioned in our publications.

To report an error: contact@xaalya.com

6. How to verify what we claim

We do not ask to be taken at our word. Each of the commitments above is verifiable:

  • No tracking tools: open your browser console (F12), 'Network' tab. The only external requests are the Tailwind CDN and your rate file. No calls to Google Analytics, no IP geolocation calls.
  • No email capture form: the comparator works without registration, without email address, without an account.
  • No affiliate links: outbound links to operators contain no affiliate tracking parameters.
  • No operator advertising: if a banner ever appears on the site, it will be labelled 'Partner' and will never concern a money transfer operator.

7. Frequently asked questions

Why not accept operator advertising, if it is identified?

Because identification is not enough. An operator paying to be present on our site, even in an identified banner, creates a commercial relationship that makes the ranking suspect.

How do you make a living if operators do not pay?

Through advertising from financial advertisers who are not money transfer operators: banks, insurers, savings fintechs, real estate players.

What happens if an operator offers a large sum?

We refuse, and we say so. This page exists precisely to make that refusal public and verifiable.

Do you collect personal data?

None. No email address, no IP, no tracking cookie, no analytics tool. Only language and theme preferences are stored, in your browser only.
